# Writing Principles

**Holds:** Voice and style rules for everything Alex writes. Channel rules go in their own files.

## Preferences
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. [confirmed 2026-03-02 · me:2026-03-02]
- Put the ask or the conclusion in the first line. [confirmed 2026-03-02]
- Friendly but direct; no exclamation marks in client email. [confirmed 2026-03-02]

## Avoid
- Buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "unlock"). [confirmed 2026-03-02]
