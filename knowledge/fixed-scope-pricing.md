# Fixed-Scope Pricing for Small Projects

**Summary:** Agree on a written scope before quoting, price the scope rather than the hours, and handle extra requests as change orders.

## Key points
- Write a one-page scope: goal, deliverables, what's out of scope, and how many revision rounds. [confirmed 2026-03-02 · me:2026-03-02]
- Quote one price for that scope. [confirmed 2026-03-02]
- Anything outside the scope becomes a small, separately priced change order. [confirmed 2026-03-02]

## When to use it
- Small client projects where the client's budget is fixed and the work is well understood.
